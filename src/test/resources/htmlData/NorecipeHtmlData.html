Grandma's Secret Chocolate Cake

By Sarah Johnson March 15, 2024
★★★★★ (127 reviews)
Prep Time: 30 minutes
Cook Time: 45 minutes
Servings: 12
Difficulty: Medium

About This Recipe

This chocolate cake recipe has been in our family for three generations. My grandmother used to make this every Sunday, and the smell would fill the entire house. It's incredibly moist, rich, and has that perfect balance of sweetness. The secret? Well, that's something you'll have to make it to find out!

Perfect for birthdays, celebrations, or just because you deserve something special. This cake pairs wonderfully with a glass of cold milk or a scoop of vanilla ice cream.

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour two 9-inch round cake pans.
  2. Step 2: In a large mixing bowl, sift together all the dry ingredients. Make sure there are no lumps!
  3. Step 3: In another bowl, whisk together the wet ingredients until well combined.
  4. Step 4: Gradually pour the wet mixture into the dry ingredients, stirring constantly to avoid lumps.
  5. Step 5: Divide the batter evenly between the prepared pans.
  6. Step 6: Bake for 40-45 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  7. Step 7: Let cool in pans for 10 minutes, then turn out onto wire racks to cool completely.
  8. Step 8: Once cooled, frost with your favorite chocolate frosting and enjoy!

Chef's Tips

  • Make sure all ingredients are at room temperature for best results
  • Don't overmix the batter - mix just until combined
  • For extra moisture, brush each layer with simple syrup before frosting
  • This cake freezes beautifully - wrap tightly and freeze for up to 3 months
